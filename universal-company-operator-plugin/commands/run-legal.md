# /run legal

Legal and compliance issue-spotting operator. This provides risk identification and checklists, not legal advice.

## Usage

```
/run legal <legal or compliance question>
```

## Workflow

1. Identify the legal/compliance area (privacy, terms, IP, employment, regulatory)
2. Spot potential issues and risks
3. Provide a risk checklist with severity levels
4. Recommend escalation points for professional legal review
5. Flag approval-gated actions (filing documents, modifying legal agreements)

## Output

Return the standard operator output: command interpreted, operators activated, assumptions, diagnosis, action plan, approval-gated actions, next step.
